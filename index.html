About AJ Toro

Run by tradespeople, not a recruitment desk.

AJ Toro Weldings was founded by a working welder. The people we put on your site are tradespeople we have worked beside for years, not names pulled from a database.

We supply experienced trades to fabrication shops, builders, head contractors and plant operators across NSW. You tell us the scope, roster and start date. We confirm who is coming, send their tickets and insurance, and stay your single point of contact until the job is done.

How it works

From enquiry to site

  1. Send the job

    Site, trades, headcount, start date, roster and duration.

  2. We confirm the crew

    Names, trades, tickets, insurance certificates and our rate, in writing.

  3. Crew mobilises

    One contact for timesheets and invoicing. If someone can't attend, we arrange cover from our network.

Before day one

Paperwork ready

  • Each tradesperson operates through their own Pty Ltd
  • Each holds their own public liability insurance
  • White Card and site tickets supplied for review
  • SWMS for hot work on request
  • Written rates and terms before mobilisation
  • Weekly timesheets and invoicing
